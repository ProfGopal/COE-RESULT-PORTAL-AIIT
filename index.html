First Time Setup

This portal uses Google Drive to store data. A one-time setup is needed to connect your Google account and create the COE folder.

1
Sign in with Google (the admin's Google account that owns the Drive)
2
The portal will create a COE folder automatically in your Drive
3
Students log in with their SEN and set their own password — stored securely in Drive

Only the admin needs to do this setup. Students just need a browser.
